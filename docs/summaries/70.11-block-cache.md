## 1. Per-connection allocations (strace -f, 1 worker thread, mmap/munmap/mremap)
Driver strace_drv.py (hard timeouts; strace as the parent of the server, killed by pid of its own child), 20 concurrent
loops of new connection -> upgrade -> CONNECT echo -> one DATA -> close.
- 64 B echo: per connection 1 x mmap+munmap 69632 (obuf: reserve(room + CHUNK) = 65539 B, a realloc from the small
  CONTINUE obuf) and 0.7 x 20480 (rbuf: 4 KiB -> RBUF_MIN 16 KiB). Total 1.72 mmap + 1.72 munmap per connection.
- 64 KiB echo: + 36864 (rbuf 32 KiB) and mremaps 20480 -> 36864 -> 69632 (rbuf doubling): 2.56 mmap + 2.56 munmap + 1.32 mremap.
- 1 MiB threshold (a): 0 mmap per connection (arena).

## 2. Variants
(a) MMAP_THRESHOLD 1 MiB: reference. Idle 165 KiB/conn (124-177). No per-connection mappings.
(c) 128 KiB threshold (the first rbuf + obuf from the arena): idle 150 KiB/conn: **rejected** (the arena pinning is back).
(b) **per-worker block cache in mem::Counting (chosen).** Freed blocks of 16..132 KiB (align <= 8, i.e. System's malloc
    path) are kept in a thread-local array (no destructor, const init, like MY_SLOT) on worker threads only: 256 slots,
    min(16 MiB, worker budget / 16) bytes (budget off: 16 MiB), sizes recorded with malloc_usable_size. alloc, realloc growth
    and alloc_zeroed (memset) take a cached block of usable size in [request, request + 8 KiB]. Flushed (free) at the end of
    each idle-sweep turn, after reap_dead; the epoll loop wakes for the sweep while the cache holds blocks, so idle memory
    falls within one sweep (<= 2 s). Cached bytes stay counted in heap_bytes (at the usable size) until flushed.
    Sizing: reap_dead frees the clients that closed in a turn at its end, the next turn's new clients take them; with 8 slots
    only 1 hit in 5 (dbg counters), with 64+ about 95%; wbench connchurn 10x10 on one worker needs up to ~100 per turn -> 256.
    ~150 lines incl. the test (mem.rs), 3 lines main.rs, ~10 lines worker.rs. No new dependency.

## 7. Expected effect / what the lead's A/B should show
- connchurn (gnu): mmap+munmap per connection 1.82 -> 0.07 (a: 0.01), so the -13% rps / +29% CPU per connection should
  mostly go; residual vs base-1.1: a memset of a 16 KiB rbuf only where alloc_zeroed hits (pressure levels), the slot scan.
- connchurn-tls: the same buffers + rustls (its buffers are < 16 KiB or Vec growth in arena): expect -5% -> ~0.
- potato download 32-128M (L3): 12.5 -> 3.3 maps per MiB (a: 2.2): most of the -7..-9% should go.
- potato echo/upload at L2: unchanged vs b12h (blocks > 132 KiB); check them in the A/B.
- musl: connchurn 1.5 -> 0.54 maps per connection.
Known limits: up to 16 MiB per worker (1/16 of its budget) held for <= 2 s after a burst of closes, counted in heap_bytes;
blocks freed on non-worker threads (DNS pool) are not cached; dirty reuse is safe (Rust never assumes zeroed memory from alloc).

## Recommendation
Take b12-fix (variant b) for base-1.2 if the VPS1/VPS2 A/B vs base-1.1 shows connchurn and potato download tie/win; idle stays 4.7.
If echo/upload potato at L2 still lose, the next step is the obuf > 132 KiB free-per-drain at L1+ (separate change).
Confidence: high on the syscall counts and the idle numbers; medium on the throughput effect (not measured, by rule).
