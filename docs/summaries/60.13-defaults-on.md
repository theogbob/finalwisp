# 60.13 Decision: credit v2 + B3 on by default (branch defaults-on, 2026-10-28; NOT on master yet)

Pointers: 20.30 (credit v2: acceptance bar, option 2, step 4), 20.31 (B3: (b)/(b2)/(iii), joint test), 60.12 (plan), 40.01.
Decision 2026-10-28: "likely default", after the real-browser run. The branch flips both defaults; it lands on master
only after a full quiet laptop A/B (step 2), then base-3.

## Evidence (both on vs both off unless noted)
- 0x49 rig (VPS2, 200 ms stalling sink): 1.51x, 0/40 killed. Fast sink 1 stream: 0.975 (200 ms) / 0.958 (20 ms), a real
  2.5-4.4% cost inside the 0.95 bar. Loopback upload ties.
- 1G pressure bench (VPS1): download+holder 3.95x, download 20x10 1.35x, echo 1.66x, echo+holder 10.7x, memory.peak echo
  391 -> 152 MiB; download 10x10 alone 1.000 tie at n=32 (pooled n=80: 0.992, a ~1% cost inside the 5% band).
- Loopback regress v2 vs v2+B3 (VPS2): 0 LOSS; upload 10x10 valid (0.961 n.s.), peak RSS 7 MiB (B3 without v2: 227 MiB).
- Browser compat (laptop, real internet, epoxy / epoxy-v2 / libcurl x 15 cases x 2 reps): default 90/90, both on 90/90.
## Known costs (accepted)
- A5: with one stream's target stalled, a sibling stream on the same connection gets 0.68 of its alone rate (base-2 0.83);
  totals equal. A3: echo memory.peak 0.76 of base-2 (bar was 0.70; heap 0.69, the rest is kernel socket memory).
- Download with B3 ~1% (pooled). Pings after CONTINUE are protocol-visible (all tested clients fine).
- B3 needs credit v2 (without it, level-0 credit moves into our heap on loopback): --no-credit-v2 with B3 on was allowed but
  not recommended (logged in 20.31). Since 60.14 B3 is off by default and refuses to run without credit v2 (startup note).
## Gate before master
Full quiet laptop A/B of defaults-on vs base-2 (lo,tls,idle; 16 reps, 60.12 CI rule), gates 25+/25+, then base-3 tag +
clean-lane reference build. A busy-laptop A/B was run first as a smoke only (no verdict).

## Outcome (2026-10-28, user option A)
The VPS A/B (see 20.31 "60.14") showed B3 costs bulk downloads under host TCP pressure (0.65-0.83, ~2.8x CPU/MiB) on both
boxes; credit v2 alone was clean on VPS2. base-3 ships credit v2 ON and B3 OFF (opt-in, requires credit v2). B3 redesign: backlog.
